{% comment %} Mock-up of a shared link's preview card, for comparing states of Open Graph data. Styles are in blog.css. Leave out `title` to show the bare URL (no Open Graph tags); `description` and `image` are optional. `image` is relative to /images/ like figure.html. layout="square" shows the compact card: the image cropped square on the left, the text beside it. layout="image" shows just the image, with `alt`, for the social images themselves: figure.html asks for a .webp of every image, and the social images have none. Alone, class="wide" caps a card at the width of a real preview; wrap several in
to lay them out in a grid. Usage: {% include posts/og-demo.html title="A post" description="Its first lines." image="og/a-post.png" caption="Own image" class="wide" %} {% endcomment %}

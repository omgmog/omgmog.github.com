A retro-heavy week over on [social.omgmog.net](https://social.omgmog.net) between 31 August and 6 September. There's a 1993 Amiga platformer getting an LLM-assisted port to Godot, HyperCard stacks running in the browser decades after anyone last opened HyperCard, and someone working out what it'd actually be like to live in a Playstation 2 scaled up to house size. Plus a proper dig into where Ganon's name really comes from, and a map of real filming locations if you fancy losing an hour to it.
